This is the whole framework as one graph. Everything is one RDF graph — "describe this system" is a query, not four separate documents.

Click any node to read what it is, what it derives from, and what it connects to. The four bands are the spec's four parts:

What — the meaning (system, domain, events, UI).
How — the realisation.
Verify — the conformance checks.
Deliver — features, versions, direction.

Tip: the amber outline marks a seam — a boundary the framework defines but does not cross.
